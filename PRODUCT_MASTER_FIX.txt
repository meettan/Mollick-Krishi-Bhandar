The previous Internal Server Error on Add Product was caused by the Product Master page/templates not being present in the running project.

This package adds:
app/masters/product_master.py
templates/masters/product_list.html
templates/masters/product_form.html
static/product_master.css

It also updates app/main.py to register the Product Master router.

Database:
localhost / root / blank password / python_login
Table:
mm_product

Run:
C:\Users\Admin\AppData\Local\Programs\Python\Python310\python.exe -m uvicorn app.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8080

Open:
http://localhost:8080/product
Then click Add Product.
